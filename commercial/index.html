Restaurant Services

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air out all shift long, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Casper HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and customers notice the drafts at the door and the heat right away. Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by written reports and service logs after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases call for.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we install during off-hours, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Inspections & Documentation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can create carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.
